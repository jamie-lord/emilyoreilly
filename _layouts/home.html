The studio

Conservation is about caring for clients' treasures — from National Trust collections to a single watercolour your grandmother left you.

I'm Emily O'Reilly. With over 25 years of experience working with paper, I've an understanding of large collections of international importance — from National collections to individual items of great sentimental value held by private clients. I'm an Accredited Conservator and a Fellow of the International Institute of Conservation: skills and knowledge assessed by fellow professionals as expert.
