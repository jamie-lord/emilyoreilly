Conservation services such as cleaning, mounting and framing, tailored to your requirements. Please telephone, email, or use the form below for advice or to arrange a visit.

If you can email a photograph of the work along with its dimensions, I should be able to give you a rough estimate of what I could do and how much it might cost.

The studio is in Barry, a short distance from Cardiff in South Wales. I'm happy to work anywhere in the UK and can arrange transport should you need it. Recently, customers have also been posting items to me to be worked on. I do not usually charge for quotes, and I'm happy to work on-site for institutions.
